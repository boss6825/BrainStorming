# Grants & Fellowships for Tech Builders

A comprehensive list of funding opportunities for student developers, indie builders, and open-source contributors. Sorted by how realistic they are for a 3rd-year B.Tech CS student in India building LLM-based projects with full-stack + DevOps skills.

---

## TIER 1: HIGH FIT — Apply to these ASAP

These are realistic, low-barrier, and many specifically target students/young builders in India or globally.

### Emergent Ventures (India Track)
- **Amount:** Flexible (modest to substantial), non-dilutive
- **Who:** Anyone 13+, entrepreneurs, students, builders, researchers
- **What:** Scalable "zero to one" ideas that meaningfully improve society
- **India Track:** Select "India" from the dropdown on the application form
- **Why apply:** Tyler Cowen runs it. Very low bureaucracy. They fund ideas, not credentials. The India track has dedicated support. People with far less experience than you have gotten this.
- **Link:** https://www.mercatus.org/emergent-ventures
- **Status:** Always open

### Build India Fellowship (Devfolio)
- **Amount:** $10,000 equity-free
- **Duration:** 6 months
- **Who:** Indian developers building public good software
- **What:** Build open-source software for India. Mentorship + network + potential hiring/investment after
- **Why apply:** Tailor-made for your profile. Open source experience + full-stack skills + LLM projects = strong fit
- **Link:** https://buildindia.devfolio.co/

### Atomic Grants (gradCapital)
- **Amount:** $2,500 no-strings-attached + $1M+ in tech credits
- **Who:** Students, recent grads, dropouts
- **What:** Independent science & engineering projects. Can lead to $200K investment if you start a company
- **Why apply:** Very low bar to entry, no-strings-attached, aimed at exactly your stage
- **Link:** https://www.gradcapital.in/atomicfellow

### 3F VC Indie Grant
- **Amount:** ₹10,000–₹15,000 one-time
- **Who:** Students under 25, individual developers
- **What:** Quick support for MVPs, hackathon projects, early experiments
- **Why apply:** Small but fast. Good first grant to get on your record. India-specific.
- **Link:** https://www.3f.vc/grants/indie

### Bellare Grants
- **Amount:** Up to ₹1 lakh INR per year
- **Who:** Anyone in India
- **What:** No-strings-attached. Covers education, side projects (software or hardware), laptop purchase, relocation for internship, etc.
- **Why apply:** Super obscure, very few people know about it. Genuinely no-strings-attached. Run by individuals, not institutions.
- **Link:** https://bellaregrants.netlify.app/

### AI Grants India (AIGI)
- **Amount:** Free API access to AI models (not cash, but removes your biggest blocker)
- **Who:** Indian developers, students, hackathon teams, indie builders
- **What:** Free access to proprietary + open-source AI model APIs. Approvals in under 48 hours.
- **Why apply:** You already have some API credits, but more never hurts. Zero friction.
- **Link:** http://aigrants.in/

### Merge Grant
- **Amount:** $100–$1,000 + $800K in credits (Stripe Atlas, Supabase, Vercel, etc.)
- **Who:** Cash-constrained young builders globally
- **What:** No-strings-attached grant for any project. 10-day average from application to funded.
- **Why apply:** Global, fast, and they've funded people from India, Kenya, Peru. Very low competition relative to YC/Thiel.
- **Link:** https://merge.club/grant
- **Status:** Open, rolling

### Young Minds Fellowship
- **Amount:** ₹1,00,000 total (₹15,000/month for 6 months + ₹10,000 graduation bonus)
- **Who:** Ages 18–23, students/recent graduates in India
- **What:** Equity-free launchpad for early-stage ventures. Pay-it-forward model.
- **Why apply:** India-specific, age-appropriate, focused on early builders
- **Link:** https://ymf.org.in/

### Fathom Grants
- **Amount:** $500–$10,000
- **Who:** Anyone building technology for learning
- **What:** Projects at any stage. Clear deliverables, managed timelines. They care about programming experience, not degrees.
- **Why apply:** If any of your LLM projects have an educational angle, this is perfect. Very few people know about this.
- **Link:** https://fathom.network/grants

### Upsurge Grants
- **Amount:** $500–$2,000
- **Who:** Solo/indie builders in AI, robotics, hardware, biotech, games
- **What:** For people building "wild, weird, ambitious things" who are really early
- **Why apply:** Explicitly for solo builders working out of bedrooms. Low competition.
- **Link:** https://grants.upsurge.io/

## TIER 2: STRONG FIT — Worth the effort

### Google Summer of Code (GSoC)
- **Amount:** $1,500 (small) / $3,000 (medium) / $6,000 (large) for India
- **Duration:** 10–22 weeks
- **Who:** Anyone 18+
- **What:** Work on open-source projects with mentorship from 700+ organizations
- **Why apply:** You already have Plone contributions. Leverage that. GSoC on your resume is gold.
- **Link:** https://summerofcode.withgoogle.com/
- **Timeline:** Applications typically Jan–Apr each year

### LFX Mentorship (Linux Foundation)
- **Amount:** $3,000–$6,600
- **Duration:** 12 weeks, 3 terms per year
- **Who:** Anyone, emphasis on open-source contributors
- **What:** Contribute to CNCF, Kubernetes, and other Linux Foundation projects
- **Why apply:** Your Docker/K8s/DevOps skills are directly relevant
- **Link:** https://mentorship.lfx.linuxfoundation.org/

### Outreachy
- **Amount:** $7,000 + $500 travel stipend
- **Duration:** 3 months, remote
- **Who:** People from underrepresented groups in tech
- **What:** Paid remote internships working on FOSS projects
- **Two cohorts:** May–August and December–March
- **Link:** https://www.outreachy.org/

### MLH Fellowship
- **Amount:** Up to $5,000 (need-based)
- **Duration:** 12 weeks
- **Who:** Aspiring software engineers
- **Tracks:** Open Source, Production Engineering (SRE), Web3
- **Why apply:** Structured mentorship, pod-based collaboration, year-round batches
- **Link:** https://fellowship.mlh.io/

### FOSS United Fellowships (India)
- **Amount:** Varies (₹1,20,000+ seen in past grants)
- **Who:** Indian developers, students, community organizers
- **What:** Fellowship grants for FOSS contributions and community work
- **Why apply:** India-specific, open-source focused, aligns with your Plone experience
- **Link:** https://platform.fossunited.org/grants/fellowships

### NLnet Foundation (NGI Zero Commons Fund)
- **Amount:** €5,000–€50,000
- **Who:** Anyone globally, individuals or organizations
- **What:** Open-source projects that contribute to the next-generation internet. Free/open license required.
- **Why apply:** Massive fund (€21.6M total). Rolling deadlines every 2 months. They fund individuals. Your DevOps/infra skills could fit well.
- **Deadline:** Next deadline: June 1, 2026
- **Link:** https://nlnet.nl/propose/

### Thiel Fellowship
- **Amount:** $250,000 over 2 years
- **Who:** Anyone 22 or younger (without a university degree requirement for application)
- **What:** Build new things instead of sitting in a classroom. No equity taken.
- **Why apply:** High-prestige, no equity. You need meaningful progress on a concrete vision, not a finished product.
- **Link:** https://thielfellowship.org/apply
- **Status:** Rolling applications

### O'Shaughnessy Fellowship
- **Amount:** Up to $100,000 equity-free
- **Duration:** 1 year
- **Who:** Builders, researchers, creatives worldwide (160+ countries)
- **What:** Projects that can "radically transform the world." Self-directed tinkerers welcome.
- **Applications close:** April 30, 2026
- **Link:** https://osvfellowship.com/

### YC Summer Fellows Grants
- **Amount:** $20,000 cash + $90,000 in cloud credits (AWS, Azure, GCP) + partner credits
- **Who:** Undergraduate CS/engineering students globally (including outside US)
- **What:** Work on your own technical projects using AI in new ways. Not expected to be commercial.
- **Why apply:** Massive value. They want "smart programmers building interesting things." Your LLM projects fit.
- **Link:** https://www.ycombinator.com/blog/summer-fellows

## TIER 3: STRETCH GOALS — Higher competition but transformative

### Z Fellows
- **Amount:** $10,000 investment at $1B valuation cap (optional, can participate without money)
- **Who:** Technical builders of all ages, globally
- **What:** 1-week intensive connecting builders with billion-dollar founders. Rolling applications.
- **Why apply:** No age restriction, global. Mentors include founders of Figma, Netflix, AngelList, DoorDash.
- **Link:** https://www.zfellows.com/

### 1517 Fund (Medici Project)
- **Amount:** $1,000+ cash grants (Venmo payment)
- **Who:** High school/college students and dropouts
- **What:** No-strings-attached. No equity. Just passion projects.
- **How to apply:** Email any team member with a 5-minute Loom video
- **Caveat:** Limited to North America for direct grants, but still worth reaching out
- **Link:** https://www.1517fund.com/medici

### Extraordinary Technical Fellowship
- **Amount:** $5,000 in OpenAI credits + $5,000+ in compute
- **Who:** 20–30 technical fellows per year
- **What:** For individuals with "extraordinary technical ability" and positive growth trajectory
- **Link:** https://www.extraordinary.dev/

### Neo Scholars
- **Amount:** Mentorship + recruiting platform + community (not direct cash)
- **Who:** College CS students
- **What:** Year-long program. Past scholars founded Cursor, Cognition (Devin), Pika, MultiOn.
- **Why apply:** Prestige + network access to top startups. OpenAI hired exclusively through Neo.
- **Link:** https://neo.com/ (watch for annual application windows)

### Interact Fellowship
- **Amount:** Sponsored retreats + project grants
- **Who:** Young technologists aged 18–23
- **What:** Retreats, seminar courses, grants for personal projects, founder support
- **Why apply:** Community + grants. They cover travel, lodging, meals.
- **Link:** https://joininteract.com/fellowship/

### Pioneer
- **Amount:** Funding + investor intros
- **Who:** Founders anywhere in the world
- **What:** Fully remote. Weekly tournaments ranked by peers + experts. Top-ranked get funded.
- **Why apply:** Fully remote, no geographic requirements. Great forcing function for weekly progress.
- **Link:** https://pioneer.app/

## STRATEGY NOTES

**What you should do right now:**

1. **Apply to Emergent Ventures (India track) today.** The application is short. Your LLM projects + full-stack skills + the "zero to one" framing is what they want. Don't overthink it.

2. **Apply to Bellare Grants.** Almost nobody knows about this. ₹1 lakh for literally anything. Email them.

3. **Apply to Merge Grant.** 10-day turnaround. $100–$1,000 + $800K in credits. Global, rolling.

4. **Apply to Atomic Grants.** $2,500 no-strings. Perfect for your stage.

5. **Get GSoC lined up for 2026/2027.** You have Plone experience. Start identifying orgs and projects now.

6. **Apply to Build India Fellowship** if you can commit 6 months to a public-good project.

7. **Apply to NLnet** if you have an open-source project idea that fits "next-generation internet" (privacy tools, decentralized infra, developer tooling).

**What makes a winning application:**

- Show what you've BUILT, not what you've learned
- A 2-minute Loom video > a 2-page essay
- Frame your LLM projects as solving real problems for real users
- Mention your open-source contributions (Plone, any others)
- Be specific: "I built X that does Y for Z people" not "I'm passionate about AI"
