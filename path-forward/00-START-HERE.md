## The whole thing in ten lines

1. **Your bottleneck is not ideas, bandwidth, or a co-founder. It's that you've never shipped and never talked to a customer.** Every other problem you named — no conviction, no tribe, no job leverage, no validation — is downstream of those two, and both are fixable in weeks.
2. **You misread Zero to One.** "Avoid competition" means monopolize a small niche, not enter empty industries. WhisperFlow launched against Siri, Alexa, and every OS's built-in dictation — by your reading, it should never have existed.
3. **KD dies with honors** — the research is unambiguous (Harvey has a Bengaluru office; solo Indian lawyers demonstrably don't pay). Archive it via a public post-mortem that becomes an asset.
4. **TNP is your most undervalued project** — the only one with a paying customer, real users, and a deadline (placement season, from Jul 25). Ship it first. It breaks the never-shipped curse.
5. **AgentYap isn't a startup; it's your distribution engine** — the job portfolio, the co-founder magnet, and the audience, all in one. Launch as-is within two weeks, then one chapter a week.
6. **Validation can't be delegated to me** — it's a transaction, not research. I prep everything (lists, scripts, pages, code); you do 15 conversations a week and ask for money. One kill-or-build verdict every Friday.
7. **Best validated-whitespace idea found: GST-notice workflow for CA firms** — surging government-clocked pain, buyers who already pay for software, no dominant player, and it's an agent workflow (your craft). Idea bank stays locked until you've shipped.
8. **The job math is better than you think:** $1000/month is the *floor* of the India-remote junior band ($1,600–2,900 is typical), and AI startups have largely replaced LeetCode with practical AI-allowed build interviews. But shipped-and-launched is the currency — so the job plan and the startup plan are the same 90 days.
9. **Do the DSA minimum anyway** (trees, graphs, stacks — 45 min/day, ~6 weeks): not for FAANG, to remove a disqualifier and keep the placement backstop cheap.
10. **ADHD becomes leverage only with mechanics:** WIP limit of 1, public deadlines, short-feedback-loop ideas (which conveniently equals profitable-from-day-one ideas), a pressure valve for shiny objects, and actual medical management.
