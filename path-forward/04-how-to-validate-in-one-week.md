# How to Validate an Idea in 7–10 Days (Without Lying to Yourself)

You asked: *"how can you come up with an idea and validate it faster, like in just one or two weeks?"* Here is the actual mechanism. It's not research. It's a structured hunt for a transaction.

---

## First principles: what makes a problem "instantly payable"

People pay **instantly** (no long sales cycle, no convincing) only when the problem sits in one of these four slots:

1. **It's already a line item.** They currently pay a human, an agency, or a worse tool to do it. You're not creating a budget, you're redirecting one. This is the single strongest filter. WhisperFlow redirected time people already "paid" every day (typing). AtoB redirected fees truckers already paid on fuel cards.
2. **There's a countable loss.** Every week of not solving it costs a number they can say out loud. (An Indian D2C seller can tell you exactly what RTO cost them last month. A CA can tell you what a missed GST deadline costs a client.)
3. **There's a deadline with teeth.** Compliance dates, filing seasons, placement season, wedding season. Deadlines convert "someday" into "this week."
4. **It's attached to revenue.** The tool helps them close/collect/sell more, and they can see the attribution.

If an idea doesn't sit in at least one slot, people will *love* it and never pay for it. Most "cool AI agent" ideas die exactly here.

Second filter, equally hard: **do YOU have access to the buyer?** Tanay had access to typers (everyone). Harshita's team went and physically hung out at truck stops. Your access map is: Indian e-commerce sellers (via QuicShop), tier-3 college placement cells (you're building for one), CA/law offices (every Indian city is full of them, and they answer their phones), students, and the online AI-agents community (via AgentYap). Ideas outside your access map cost 10x more to validate. Start inside the map.

## The 7-day protocol

**Day 0 — Pick ONE niche + problem hypothesis.** One sentence: "*[Specific person] loses [specific money/time] every [period] because [problem], and currently handles it by [current painful method].*" If you can't fill in "currently handles it by," stop — no current behavior means no current budget.

**Day 0, same day — I do the prep (this is where you use me):**
- Build a list of 30 reachable targets (names, firms, numbers/handles). For sellers: Shopify/Dukaan/Instagram sellers, QuicShop's segment. For colleges: TPO contact pages. For CAs: Google Maps + directories.
- Draft the outreach message (WhatsApp/LinkedIn/call script) — short, asks about *their current process*, never pitches.
- Draft the 6-question interview script (below).

**Days 1–4 — You do 10–15 conversations.** Calls or in-person beat chat. The script is Mom-Test style — never ask "would you use X?" (everyone lies to be polite). Ask only about the past and present:
1. "Walk me through the last time [problem] happened. What did you do?"
2. "What does that cost you — money, hours, staff?"
3. "What have you already tried or paid for to fix it?" ← **the money question. If the answer is 'nothing,' the pain is not real, whatever they say next.**
4. "Who else deals with this worse than you?"
5. "If this disappeared tomorrow, what changes for you?"
6. (Only if 1–5 were hot) "I'm building something for exactly this. It'll be ₹X/month. Can I set you up as one of the first three, starting [date]?" — and then **ask for the advance / a token payment / a signed WhatsApp 'yes.'**

**Day 5 — Score it, mechanically:**
- 8+ of 15 describe the problem unprompted and can name its cost → pain confirmed.
- 5+ already pay something (human/tool/jugaad) to cope → budget confirmed.
- **3+ say yes to a paid pilot (money or written commitment) → BUILD.**
- Anything less → write a 5-line post-mortem, next hypothesis Monday. This is a win, not a failure — you killed a loser in one week instead of three months. You've already paid three months' tuition on KD to learn what skipping this step costs.

**Days 6–10 — Build only the sliver the 3 payers need.** Not the platform. The sliver. Deliver it half-manually if needed (you + Claude behind the curtain is a legitimate v1 — "concierge MVP"). Charging for a service you partly perform by hand is validation *and* revenue.
