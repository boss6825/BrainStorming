# The 90-Day Plan (July 16 → October 15, 2026)

Your real constraints, stated honestly: internship 9–6 on weekdays, college restarting July 25, placement season, evenings (~6:30–11pm) and weekends as your build time, ADHD that kills projects at the 80% wall, and three unfinished projects. The plan is built *around* these, not in denial of them. One active project at a time ([WIP limit = 1](08-adhd-mechanics.md)); everything else is either background-cadence or parked.

The plan serves three goals **simultaneously** — this is the point. Shipping TNP + AgentYap is *also* the job portfolio ([06](06-job-strategy.md)) and *also* the co-founder magnet ([07](07-cofounders-and-people.md)). You are not splitting your bandwidth three ways; the same 90 days compound into all three.

## Phase 1 — Break the curse (Jul 16 → Aug 15)

The single objective of this phase: **go from "person with 3 unfinished projects" to "person who ships."** Everything downstream depends on this identity flip.

**Active project: TNP.**
- Define "done" now, in writing: students can register + upload profiles, TPO can manage drives, recruiters can browse — deployed, and the TPO has announced it to students. Nothing more. Cut every feature not needed for *this* season.
- Target: live by **Aug 10** (college opens Jul 25; drives ramp after). This deadline has teeth and witnesses — your ADHD's favorite kind.
- Collect the ₹20–40k. Actually invoice. Getting paid is part of the rep you're building.

**Background (strictly time-boxed):**
- **AgentYap launch — two evenings, before Jul 31.** Domain, deploy, your name + X handle on every page, one launch thread on X/LinkedIn. As-is. Done > perfect. Then it goes to background cadence: one chapter/thread per week, ≤4 hrs, weekend slot.
- **Build-in-public starts now:** 3–4 posts/week on X. What you shipped, what broke. Two minutes a post. This is the compounding asset behind the job search AND the co-founder search.

**Parked, with park-notes:** KD (until its week), the idea bank (locked).

**KD post-mortem week (Aug 8–15):** write the "why I'm shutting down my legal AI startup" chapter for AgentYap ([per the audit](02-project-audit.md)), salvage reusable modules, archive the repo. If — only if — you choose the optional 10-lawyer validation pass, it happens this week and the verdict executes by Aug 15 regardless. No zombie state after Aug 15.

**Also in this phase:** DSA starts — 45 min/day, trees first (Jul 16–31), then graphs (Aug). Morning slot before the internship if possible; it should never touch build-time. It's a disqualifier-remover, not a pathway ([06](06-job-strategy.md)).

## Phase 2 — First real validation (Aug 15 → Sep 15)

TNP is live and running through season (maintenance + fixes only — real users will generate the bug list; fixing fast for them is customer development, feel it). Now the active slot opens for the thing you've never done: **the validation loop.**

- **Week of Aug 17: run the [one-week protocol](04-how-to-validate-in-one-week.md) on the [CA-firm GST-notice idea](05-idea-bank.md).** I prep the target list of 30 CA firms, scripts, and outreach drafts the weekend before; you do 15 conversations across the week (calls/visits — CA offices are everywhere and they answer phones). Verdict Friday: 3 paid pilots → build the sliver; fewer → post-mortem, next hypothesis Monday.
- If it validates: **Sep = build the concierge MVP for the 3 paying firms.** Half-manual delivery is legitimate and desirable — maximum learning, minimum build.
- If it doesn't: run the loop on hypothesis #2 (TNP expansion — pitch 8 nearby colleges with your live case study) and, in parallel, log QuicShop/placement-season observations into `observations.md` for hypothesis #3.
- Either way, by Sep 15 you will have done more real validation than in your entire founder life so far, and — this matters for your motivation wiring — possibly collected your first non-contract revenue.

**Background:** AgentYap weekly cadence continues. First hackathon (pick one Devfolio listing, one weekend, treat as sacred — it's co-founder hunting disguised as fun). DSA continues (graphs → stacks/queues).

## Phase 3 — Convert (Sep 15 → Oct 15)

By now you have: a live college platform mid-season, a launched AgentYap with 6–8 chapters and weekly threads, a KD post-mortem that shows judgment, possibly paying CA pilots, and an X trail. That's a *portfolio*. Phase 3 spends it.

- **Job engine on ([06-job-strategy.md](06-job-strategy.md)):** 10 personalized YC Work-at-a-Startup applications/week, Wellfound batches, HN "Who is hiring" on Oct 1, 5 warm founder DMs/week. Positioning: "engineer who builds and writes about AI agents," with links.
- **Interview training:** 4–6 mock "build under observation, AI allowed" sessions with me. Schedule them like exams.
- **Placement season backstop:** sit for pre-filtered on-campus companies only ([bar defined in 06](06-job-strategy.md)). An offer in hand is cheap insurance.
- **Startup thread continues:** deepen whichever validated — CA pilots toward ₹10–15k MRR, or TNP toward 2–3 more colleges. Small numbers, real money, compounding proof.

## What the board looks like on Oct 15 (realistic, not fantasy)

- 2 launched products with real users; 1 honest public post-mortem; possibly ₹5–40k of non-salary revenue and 2–3 paying pilot customers.
- A job funnel running with a portfolio that converts, plus a placement backstop.
- 8+ weeks of public building — the tribe forming around it, hackathon #1 and #2 done.
- DSA gaps closed to "not a disqualifier."
- A trained validation muscle and an `observations.md` full of firsthand pain.

None of these is the whole dream. All of them are the dream's actual prerequisites, and every one is reachable in evenings and weekends — because the plan never asks you for more than **one active project, a weekly content cadence, and 45 min/day of DSA.**

## Weekly operating rhythm (the whole system on one line each)

- **Mon–Fri 6:30–11pm:** active project only. Fixed location, fixed opening ritual ([08](08-adhd-mechanics.md)).
- **Morning 45 min:** DSA (through Sep), then interview prep (Oct).
- **Sat:** deep build block + AgentYap chapter.
- **Sun evening, 30 min, non-negotiable:** review — did the active project move? what ships this week? one X post declaring it. This half-hour is the whole accountability system.
- **Any moment a shiny new idea appears:** 30 minutes to write it into the idea bank, then back ([08](08-adhd-mechanics.md), rule 4).
